📊 What is a stock?

A stock represents a small ownership share in a company. Its price may rise or fall over time.

🌍 Diversification

Learning about different industries and markets can help you understand why people avoid relying on a single company.

⚠️ Risk

Investments can lose value. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

⏳ Long-term thinking

Investing is often studied over long periods rather than focusing only on short-term changes.
